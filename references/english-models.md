# English explainers as question-order maps

After S1 and S2, fetch 1–3 English explainers about the same object: a textbook passage, education page, institutional explainer, or news explanation of the current paper.

Record only:

1. URL and source type;
2. `reader question → primary evidence that answers it → next question`;
3. conflicts with S1;
4. material excluded from the article.

English sources decide only what a non-specialist needs to ask next. Facts still come from primary `[n]` sources. Their words, clause structure, sentence length, rhythm, and transitions are not Chinese drafting material.

After recording the question order, draft from verified S1 facts and the bundled Chinese-writing references. An optional Chinese-revision integration may be used, but it is not required. Do not import modern teaching notation into a historical voice, promotional openings, abstract compression, or counts that conflict with the primary source.
