# Reader voice

The speaker is a careful reader of papers talking to a smart non-specialist.

Keep retrieval traces, DOI checks, claim boundaries, and drafting notes in `runs/`. Reader prose should not announce that a source was checked, explain what the outline permits, or repeat tool output.

Cold-read every sentence:

- Who acted, observed, measured, or judged?
- Does the sentence preserve natural Chinese rather than mirror foreign clause order?
- Is a short sentence present only to force a transition?
- Is a note aimed at an editor rather than a reader?
- Does the caption identify the image and provenance before any necessary clarification?

When a paragraph fails, rewrite it from verified facts.

The final section should connect the historical work to a current paper on the same core object, and, when documented sources exist, to reception history. Do not end with an academic boundary paragraph or unsupported praise.
