# Voice and title contract

- Visible title: `生物学名人系列｜人物：具体问题`.
- The question must be understandable to a non-specialist on first reading.
- Internal filenames may use `BG-XXX`; visible titles, headings, body text, and reader-file image destinations must not.
- `series_class` is `famous` or `non-famous`.
- Famous target: 3500–5000 Hanzi when sources support it.
- Ordinary target: 2500–3600 Hanzi, with a 2000-Hanzi floor.
- Never pad with awards, praise, CV details, repeated ratios, or unsupported context.
- Open with the problem rather than the person's prestige.
- Keep dates, numbers, titles, attribution, and results traceable to `[n]`.
- Do not back-project later genes, molecules, or notation into a historical actor's vocabulary.
- Use bounded verbs such as 提出、观察到、支持、与……一致 and 留下问题.
- The reader file contains events and explanations, not research logs, boundary tables, or mechanical-gate language.
