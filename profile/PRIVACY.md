# Privacy and neutrality limits / 隐私和中立的边界

A profile of a researcher is a tool for a decision about work. It is not a file on a person.

研究者档案是做工作决定的工具，不是一个人的档案袋。

## Allowed / 可以收集

- Public professional information: positions, degrees, training, papers, talks, grants, awards, the lab page, the institution page, published interviews and the researcher’s own writing.
- Public records of the research record: Crossref notices, Retraction Watch data as carried by Crossref, and public comments on PubPeer that you read by hand.
- Contact routes that the lab or institution publishes for enquiries.
- Public personal background that the person or a named outlet made public, about the path and public roles: interviews, autobiographies, public talks, lab or institution pages, profiles in named outlets, and the person’s own public accounts. Each item says who said it and when, and is labelled Self-stated or Reported by.
- Public accounts that an official page ties to the person, read only as far as they are visible without signing in: type of content, bio, date of the last post.
- Public reactions by named people and outlets, and formal documents (court, institution, funder, publisher), each with who and when.

公开的职业信息：职位、学位、训练、论文、报告、经费、奖项、课题组页面、机构页面、公开发表的访谈和本人写的文章；对科研记录的公开记录；课题组或机构为咨询而公布的联系方式；本人或署名媒体公开的、关于经历和公共角色的个人背景（访谈、自传、公开报告、课题组或机构页面、署名媒体的人物报道、本人的公开账号），每条写明谁说的、何时，并标"自述"或"报道"；官方页面能对应到本人的公开账号，只读不登录就能看到的部分（内容类型、简介、最近一条的日期）；署名的人和媒体的公开反应，以及正式文件（法院、机构、基金机构、出版社），各写明是谁、何时。

## Not allowed / 不收集

- Anything non-public. Anything behind a login, a paywall you do not have, a security check, or a private group. Do not sign in, do not use someone else’s account or session, do not go around a block, do not scrape private or locked accounts.
- Home address, personal phone, personal e-mail, family members, partner, children, health, religion, politics, immigration status, money and pay of a named person, age or birthday when it is not part of a published biography. A family detail that appears in a public autobiography is still left out of the profile.
- Background-check work: no property, court-record, employment-history or contact-trace searches on a person, no tracing of accounts that no official page ties to the person, no linking of a person’s accounts across sites by guessing. This is not a tool to find a person who does not want to be found.
- Photos of private life. Social media posts that are not about work or public roles.
- Trainees: no individual profiles, no grading of named students, no private messages shown to others.
- Guesses: no inference about character, motive or private reasons from a record, a silence or a move. Anonymous claims, rumors and unverified allegations are not repeated.

任何非公开内容；需要登录、付费墙、安全验证或私密群才能看的内容，不要登录、不借用他人账号或会话、不绕过拦截、不抓取私密或加锁的账号；家庭住址、私人电话和邮箱、家人、伴侣、子女、健康、宗教、政治、身份状况、金钱和具体某人的薪酬、非公开传记里的年龄和生日；公开自传里出现的家庭细节，同样不写进档案；背景调查式的工作（查房产、法院记录、工作经历、联系方式，追踪官方页面没有对应到本人的账号，靠猜把一个人在各站的账号串起来），这不是用来找不愿被找到的人的工具；私人生活照片和与工作、公共角色无关的社交媒体；学生：不做个人档案，不给具名学生评分，不展示私下的消息；猜测：不从记录、沉默或调动去推断性格、动机或私人原因，匿名说法、传闻和未经核实的指控不转述。

## Ordinary people and thin records / 普通人和资料很少的情况

Most researchers are not famous. A graduate student or a new assistant professor may have a lab page, an ORCID record and a few papers, and nothing else. Check the public social media and institutional pages anyway, because that is often all there is. When little is public, say so plainly in the profile ("little public information: only the professional record is public") and keep the profile short. Do not pad it with guesses, with same-name results, or with material from non-public places. A short honest profile is a good result.

大多数研究者不是名人。研究生或新任助理教授可能只有一个课题组页面、一条 ORCID 记录和几篇论文。公开的社交媒体和机构页面仍然要查，因为常常只有这些。公开的很少，就在档案里直说（"公开信息很少：只有职业记录是公开的"），档案保持简短。不要用猜测、同名结果或来自非公开渠道的材料凑数。简短而诚实的档案就是好结果。

## Reactions are reactions / 反应只是反应

Community reactions (PubPeer, Retraction Watch, news, blogs, forums, public social commentary) are recorded as what a named person or outlet said, with the date and address. They are neither findings nor verdicts. Praise and criticism get the same wording. A dispute between institutions (for example over a patent) is a formal item and is not about the person’s conduct unless the document says so. Allegations that no formal body has reported are not repeated.

社区反应（PubPeer、Retraction Watch、新闻、博客、论坛、公开社交评论）只记为署名的人或媒体说了什么，带日期和地址，既不是发现也不是裁决。赞扬和批评用同样的措辞。机构之间的纠纷（例如专利）算"正式"项，除非文件本身这样写，否则与这个人的行为无关。没有正式机构报告过的指控不转述。

## Neutral wording / 中立的写法

- Quote or closely paraphrase the source, with its address and date.
- For a notice: type, issuer, DOI, date, and the reason as the notice gives it.
- For a disagreement or a dispute: say that the source reports it and who the parties are; do not take a side.
- Do not use words such as fraud, fake, toxic or misconduct unless the quoted text of an official finding uses them, and then put them in quotation marks with the issuer name.
- Anonymous claims are not facts and are not repeated.

引用或贴近转述来源，附地址和日期；对通告写类型、发布者、DOI、日期和通告自己写明的原因；对争议只写来源报道了什么、各方是谁，不选边；除非官方结论的原文用了这些词，否则不用"造假""欺诈""恶劣"这类词，用了就加引号并注明发布者；匿名说法不是事实，不转述。

## If you find something sensitive by accident / 意外看到敏感内容

Do not copy it. Leave it out of the profile and out of notes. If it is about a safety matter or a formal complaint that affects you, use the institution’s official channels rather than the profile.

不要复制，不写进档案和笔记。如果涉及安全问题或与你有关的正式投诉，走机构的正式渠道，不要写进档案。

## Sharing / 分享

Profiles of real people stay in your own workspace (`work/` is not committed). Share only with the people helping you decide, and not with the researcher unless they ask. The worked example in examples/ is built only from public pages and records, and is a demonstration of the method, not an assessment of the person.

真人的档案留在自己的工作区（`work/` 不提交）。只分享给帮你做决定的人，除非研究者提出，否则不给本人看。examples/ 里的案例只用公开页面和记录，是方法演示，不是对这个人的评价。
