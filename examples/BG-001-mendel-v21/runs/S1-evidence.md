# S1 evidence summary

## Research checkpoint

- Core object: counts for Mendel's seven pairs of pea traits, not a monastery biography.
- Primary source: Mendel 1866, DOI `10.5962/bhl.title.61004`.
- Reception: Kottler 1979 and Fisher 1936 distinguish limited early citation from sustained research use.
- Statistical dispute: Fisher raised fit and reconstruction questions; Hartl and Fairbanks warn that statistical concern is not proof of fabrication.
- Recent high-profile search: one original core-object paper was identified in the prior 24 months, Feng et al. 2025 *Nature* 642:980–989. Ross 2025 *Nature Plants* is a commentary.
- Boundary: molecular mechanisms and modern association methods are later work, not Mendel's vocabulary.
