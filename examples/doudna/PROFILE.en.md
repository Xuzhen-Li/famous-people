# Profile: Jennifer A. Doudna

Prepared: 2026-11-26, China Standard Time. Purpose: a worked example of the method, using a well-known public scientist. It shows how the profile is built and what its limits are. It is not an assessment of the person and not a recommendation.
Scope: public information only (professional record, public personal background, public presence, public reactions). See PRIVACY.md. Every line below ends with a source tag such as [S3], or says unknown. If little public information exists, say so plainly and keep the profile short: do not pad it, and do not search beyond public professional and public-presence sources.

## 9. Public personal background

- Self-stated: she grew up on the island of Hawaii, where the family moved when she was seven (August 1971); she says feeling like an outsider at school "drove me to take risks and prove doubters wrong" [S7].
- Self-stated: she says a high school guidance counselor "strongly discouraged" her from a chemistry major, and that she went to Pomona College anyway [S7].
- Self-stated: public roles outside the lab include founding and presiding over the Innovative Genomics Institute, speaking with US senators and taking part in public discussion of how genome editing should be regulated, and starting a coronavirus testing laboratory in March 2020 [S7].
- Reported by C&EN: a profile dated 2026-02-23 carries the subtitle that she "struggled with finding her way through school" and opens with her childhood on the Big Island of Hawaii; only the opening of the article was readable without signing in [S16].
